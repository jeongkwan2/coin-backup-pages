Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Application Purpose

Coin Auto Backup is a personal application for backing up and restoring cryptocurrency trading bot configuration and operational data.

2. Google Drive Access

The application accesses Google Drive to upload, download, and manage encrypted backup files.

3. Data Storage

Backup files are encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive.

4. Data Sharing

The application is intended for personal use. Google Drive data is not sold or shared with third parties.

5. Data Retention

Backup files are retained according to the user's configured backup policy. Files may be deleted by the user.

6. Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account settings.
